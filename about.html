ABOUT EBWATER

EBWater provides independent water safety advice, supporting healthcare organisations in understanding, managing and governing water-related risks.

EBWater helps healthcare organisations complete the picture in healthcare water safety by bridging the gap between engineering controls, water safety requirements and the clinical realities of patient care.

Drawing on extensive experience in Infection Prevention and Control, EBWater translates complex guidance, standards, and risks into practical actions that improve patient safety, support compliance, and enhance operational effectiveness.

Clinical Experience

  • Over 25 years in clinical practice
  • 18 years specialising in Infection Prevention and Control
  • Expertise in identifying and mitigating healthcare-associated infection risks, underpinned by quality improvement methodology
  • IPC Education and Research lead
  • Clinical Leadership Fellow, East Midlands Clinical Senate

Evidence-Based Approach

  • National Institute for Health Research funded Master's in Health Research
  • Committed to an evidence-based, scientifically rigorous approach to healthcare water safety
  • Skilled in translating legislation, standards, guidance, and emerging evidence into practical policies, risk assessments, audit reports, technical documentation, and training materials
  • Invited speaker at professional conferences and author of numerous articles supporting evidence-based healthcare practice
