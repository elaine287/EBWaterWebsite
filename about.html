ABOUT EBWATER

EBWater provides independent water safety and risk consultancy, supporting organisations in understanding, managing and governing water-related risks.

Clinical & Strategic Experience

26 years in clinical practice, with 18 years specialising in Infection Prevention and Control (IPC).

Clinical Leadership

From my experience as IPC Water Safety Lead and IPC Responsible Person for Water Safety, I have expert knowledge of the risks posed by water and wastewater, and the engineering controls to reduce this risk. Crucially, I translate these controls to the clinical setting with maximum impact to promote patient safety. My clinical grounding brings clinical teams on board with the water safety agenda.

Strategic Governance

As an active member of technical, operational, and strategic water safety groups, I have an in-depth understanding of all levels of water safety governance. During my 2 years as a Clinical Leadership Fellow with the East Midlands Clinical Senate, I strengthened skills in cross-organizational collaboration across complex systems, change management, and delivering data-driven strategic advice.

Key Experience Areas

  • Writing and reviewing water safety documents to align with healthcare legislation and guidance
  • Translating engineering and design controls into safe clinical practices, including 'water-lite' strategies
  • Significant experience of Pseudomonas aeruginosa clinical outbreaks and outlet contamination
  • Auditing, education, risk assessment and risk management
  • Leading initiatives to improve water safety, with patient safety as the key focus

Evidence-Based Approach

Having completed a prestigious National Institute for Health Research funded Master's in Health Research, I possess an evidence-based, scientifically rigorous approach to healthcare water safety.


My advanced academic training empowers me to expertly assemble and present complex data, translating these into clear, actionable safety strategies that give healthcare facilities ultimate confidence in patient safety. I have also published numerous articles and been an active part of many clinical networks.
